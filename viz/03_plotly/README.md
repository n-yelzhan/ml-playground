\# Plotly basics — California Housing

Goal: explore what drives house prices across California districts with interactive plotly charts (histogram, scatter with hover, map by coordinates).

Model: no model — exploratory analysis on sklearn California Housing (1990 census).

Result: price grows with median income; coastal districts (San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles) are the most expensive. 1011 districts are capped at $500k.

Key takeaway: find caps and units before analysis — they distort averages and correlations, and removing them is also a decision that changes results.
