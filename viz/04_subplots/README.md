\# Subplots and presentation style — Tips

Goal: build one 2x2 presentation-ready figure that summarizes restaurant bills and tips.

Model: no model — matplotlib subplots + seaborn on the Tips dataset, saved as PNG for slides.

Result: one slide with 4 charts in a single style; tip is a stable \~16% of the bill across days and lunch/dinner.

Key takeaway: a chart becomes a slide when its title states the conclusion, not just what is plotted.
